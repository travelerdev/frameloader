How to get loading

  1. 1
    Turn on Developer ModeSteam Settings → System → Enable Developer Mode, then set a user password under Developer.
  2. 2
    PairOpen Settings → Developer → Pair new host on the headset, click Pair in Frameloader, and approve.
  3. 3
    DropFrom then on it's drag, drop, Install. The title appears under Non-Steam in your library.

Frameloader automates the sideloading process that Valve built for developers to test apps on the Frame. This lets you get apps into the Frame's library without needing to mess around with the terminal yourself every time.

Actually private - no analytics, no tracking, nothing.

Frameloader is a tiny piece of freeware that solves one problem - getting apps from your computer to your Steam Frame.

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting. Frameloader doesn't count you, profile you, or phone home.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
  • Talks only to your Frame. Its only connections are to the headset on your own network: SSH, Valve's pairing service, and local discovery.
  • This website doesn't track you either. No cookies, no analytics, no third-party fonts or scripts.

Don't take our word for it: read the code.
